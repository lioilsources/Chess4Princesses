Chess Kingdom teaches little children chess through play. Every piece is a fairy-tale character: the bridesmaids only walk forward, the Rook drives straight like a little train, the Butterfly flies on the slant, the Pony jumps in an L and the Princess can do it all.

• Short story lessons with puzzles: collect stars, avoid the lava, save the King.
• A narrator reads every line aloud (in English and Czech), so even children who can't read yet can play.
• Cheerful piece battles instead of plain captures.
• Two armies: Fairies and Witches.
• Stars for every lesson and clear progress.
• No ads, no tracking, works offline.

For children aged roughly 4 to 8 and their parents.
